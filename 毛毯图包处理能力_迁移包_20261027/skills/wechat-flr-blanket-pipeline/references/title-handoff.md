# Title Handoff

This skill does not replace title generation rules.

Before generating titles, use:

- `temu-blanket-title`
- `temu-blanket-batch-title`

Current important title constraints:

- `Flannel Blanket` must appear within the first 5 words.
- `280GSM` must appear exactly once.
- Title length must be under 250 characters.
- Do not use child/minor wording.
- Do not use bed-use wording such as `bed`, `bedroom`, `bedding`, `bedspread`, or `bed cover`.
- Prefer fuller scene phrases such as `For Sofa Living Room Office Travel Car` or `For Couch Living Room Office Travel Camping Car`.

For text printed in images, describe the safe theme instead of copying risky words directly.
