# Compliance Rules

Apply these rules before outputting the title.

## Banned or Risky Terms

Do not use:
- `3D`
- `2D`
- `wool`
- `cotton`
- `sherpa`
- `fleece`
- `100% polyester`
- `polyester`
- `plush`
- `thick`
- `heavy`
- `hypoallergenic`
- `allergy-friendly`
- `skin-friendly`
- `medical`
- `therapeutic`
- `pet blanket`
- `bed`
- `bedding`
- `bedspread`
- `bedtime`
- `night's sleep`
- `night sleep`
- `sleeping`
- `sleep`
- `bedroom`
- `bed cover`

Use `Flannel` as the only explicit material word.

## Bed-Use Restriction

Do not promote the blanket for bed use. Avoid wording that positions it as bedding or a bed accessory:
- `bed`
- `bedding`
- `bedspread`
- `bedtime`
- `night's sleep`
- `night sleep`
- `sleeping`
- `sleep`
- `bedroom`
- `bed cover`

Use broader home and travel scenarios instead:
- `Sofa`
- `Couch`
- `Living Room`
- `Office`
- `Travel`
- `Car`
- `Camping`
- `Nap`
- `Lounge`

## Children and Minor-Related Terms

Do not use child/minor terms, including case variants, singular/plural variants, and spacing variants:
- `crib`
- `kids`
- `kid`
- `child`
- `children`
- `baby`
- `babies`
- `toddler`
- `toddlers`
- `infant`
- `infants`
- `youngster`
- `youngsters`
- `boys`
- `boy`
- `girls`
- `girl`
- `nursery`
- `school`
- `student`
- `students`
- `classmates`
- `granddaughter`
- `grandson`
- `cot`
- `babybed`
- `baby bed`

If the image text contains minor-related relationships or school/classmate terms, rewrite safely using:
- `Family Gift`
- `Holiday Gift`
- `Love Message Gift`
- `Gift For Family Friends`

## IP, Brand, and Trademark Risk

Do not use:
- Brand names.
- Character names.
- Movie, anime, game, or comic names.
- Celebrity names.
- Team names.
- Trademark-like or logo-like terms.

Describe the visible subject generically instead.

Examples:
- Use `Space Theme`, not a protected space franchise phrase.
- Use `Cute Mouse`, not a protected character name.
- Use `Superhero Style`, only if generic and not tied to a protected hero.

## Platform Residue

Remove meaningless copied text such as:
- `Open`
- `Launch`
- `mall link`
- raw URLs
- copied platform/navigation words

## Liability and Unsupported Claims

Avoid claims that are difficult to prove or easy to dispute:
- allergy claims
- medical claims
- skin claims
- guaranteed durability claims
- exact material percentages

Safe phrases:
- `Soft`
- `Warm`
- `Cozy`
- `Comfortable`
- `Lightweight`
- `All Season`
- `Machine Washable`
- `Digital Print`
- `Home Decor`
- `Gift`

## Human-Use Product Rule

The product is a blanket for people. Do not describe it as a pet blanket or pet bedding.
For animal-themed products, use safe phrases such as:
- `Dog Lover Gift`
- `Cat Lover Gift`
- `Animal Print`
- `Pet Lover Gift`
