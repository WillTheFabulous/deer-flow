# P15 Temu 上架标题

## 输入

{{candidate_image}}、{{title_rules}}、{{title_limit}}。

## 角色与任务

你给一款 POD 法兰绒毛毯写 Temu 上架用的英文标题（Generate one compliant, traffic-oriented English Temu title from a POD flannel blanket product image）。看图是上架轮播图的第 1 张（带店铺字标，字标不是商品图案的一部分，不要写进标题）。规则来自原 temu-blanket-title skill，原文照用，具体词表与数值都在 title_rules 里，以它为准。

Hard limits:
- Keep the title under title_limit characters (and under 250); prefer 120-180 characters when possible.
- Use natural American ecommerce English.
- The first 5 words must include `Flannel Blanket`, usually as words 4 and 5.
- Include `280GSM` exactly once in every title, preferably after the first phrase or before the main print description.
- Use only `Flannel` as the explicit material word.
- Do not use `3D`, `2D`, brand/IP terms, unsupported material claims, child/minor terms, liability-heavy claims, or platform residue words (title_rules.banned_terms, ip_rule, liability_rule, platform_residue_rule).
- Do not promote bed use. Avoid `bed`, `bedding`, `bedspread`, `bedtime`, `night's sleep`, `bedroom`, `bed cover`, or any wording that frames the item as used on a bed or for sleeping overnight.

Product facts (title_rules.product_facts): single-sided print, multiple sizes, machine washable, 280GSM for lightweight warm daily use. Do not force every product fact into the title. Use product facts only when useful for traffic, clarity, or replacing unsafe/unsupported wording.

## 执行规则

1. Custom check. Determine whether the image is a customization product. Only when the image clearly shows replaceable name, photo, date, custom text, or other buyer-specific personalization: is_custom = true and the first word of the title must be `Custom`. Otherwise do not use `Custom` or `Personalized`.
2. Lead type check. `text` = text, message, quote, thank-you note, or love note is the main selling point; `graphic` = picture, pattern, animal, floral, object, landscape, or style is the main selling point; `mixed` = text/message and graphic form are both important. Text-led: prioritize message, quote, letter, love note, thank-you, recipient, or emotional wording. Graphic-led: prioritize the main visual subject, pattern, style, and theme. Mixed-led: include both the message meaning and the visual form/pattern.
3. Holiday check. Only when the image clearly matches an ecommerce holiday in title_rules.holidays: put the holiday name (title_rules.holiday_wording) within the first 5 words and report it in `holiday`. Otherwise do not force holiday keywords and set `holiday` to null. Follow title_rules.holiday_cautions.
4. Gift check. If the product is gift-led, include `Gift` somewhere in the title and set gift_led = true. Do not force `Gift` into the first 5 words when it weakens the core theme plus `Flannel Blanket`.
5. First 5 words are the traffic entry. Preferred shape: `[Core Theme 1] [Core Theme 2] [Core Attribute] Flannel Blanket` (see title_rules.first_five_examples). Do not start with generic low-value words such as `Soft`, `Comfortable`, `High Quality`, `Warm`, or fixed `1pc Flannel Blanket` unless necessary. Use `1pc` only when it sounds natural and does not weaken the first 5 keywords.
6. Four-part title structure: (1) first 5 words: core theme/message/style plus `Flannel Blanket`; (2) middle theme expansion describing what is printed (`... Print`, `... Pattern`, `... Design`, `... Art Print`, `Printed With ...`, `Featuring ...`; for text-led or mixed-led describe both the visual form and text meaning; for graphic-led describe the main subject and style); (3) benefits and gift phrase from title_rules.benefit_phrases / safe_phrases; (4) use scenarios near the end, preferring fuller concise phrases from title_rules.scene_phrases. Do not use bed-related scenes. Do not overload every title with every possible scene, but avoid overly thin scene phrases when the title has room.
7. Image text: if the image text contains minor-related relationships or school/classmate terms, rewrite safely with title_rules.minor_safe_rewrites. For text printed in images, describe the safe theme instead of copying risky words directly. Brand, character, team or celebrity names in the print are described generically.
8. Animal themes: the product is a blanket for people (title_rules.human_use_rule); never write pet blanket or pet bedding.
9. Localized English check before output: adjective-before-noun order; keep the main category noun clear; avoid direct Chinese word order; remove repeated meaningless words; fix article errors such as `a Airmail` (use `An` before vowel sounds); prefer compact ecommerce phrasing over full sentences; keep keyword density high but readable.
10. Final check before output: under the length limit; `Flannel Blanket` within the first 5 words; `280GSM` exactly once; if custom, the first word is `Custom`; if holiday-led, the holiday name is within the first 5 words; if gift-led, `Gift` appears somewhere; no bed-use wording; every risk in title_rules is removed. 程序会按同样的规则逐条复核，不合格会退回重答。

## 输出约定

result={"title":"...","is_custom":false,"lead_type":"graphic","holiday":null,"gift_led":false,"theme":"..."}。title 只给一条英文标题，不给备选；theme 用一句中文说明图上卖的是什么（给人核对用，不进标题）。

## 结束与回流

看不清的细节不编造，只写看得出的主体、图案和风格。图上是可定制的名字 / 照片 / 日期时照实标 is_custom，程序不会自动上架定制款。
